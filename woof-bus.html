The Woof Bus

A dedicated bark and ride for very important pooches

Bark and ride — V.I.P's only (Very Important Pooches)

Travelling in style

The safety and comfort of your four-legged friend, while travelling to and from day care and their walkies, is hugely important to us. So we designed the inside of the Woof Bus from scratch to make every journey as comfortable — and as high a standard — as it possibly can be.

Door-to-door collection means no crated car journeys, no juggling the school run, and no rushing back for pick-up. Your dog gets a calm, familiar ride with their pals, and you get your morning back.

Let's look inside

  • Soothing lavender scents throughout
  • Custom-fitted crates of the highest quality, with escape doors
  • Made-to-measure memory foam orthopaedic beds
  • Temperature control, to keep things just right
  • Secure and safe for any size of dog
  • All-round panoramic windows to enjoy the view
  • Full central locking
  • Up-and-over rear doors, for shelter from the rain while towelling off

And a few extras

Because a Very Important Pooch travels properly equipped.

1

Their own drying robe

Every dog has a drying robe of their own on board — so muddy paws and wet coats never make it onto your sofa.

2

Toys & leads aboard

Plenty of toys for the journey, and spare leads always to hand, so nothing ever gets forgotten at home.

3

Familiar faces

The same friendly driver and the same travel companions every day — a calm, settled routine your dog knows well.

Pick-up & drop-off

We always do our best to work around what suits you.

  • Morning collections between 8am and 9am
  • Home again between 4pm and 5pm

£5 each way — so £10 for a collection and a drop-off.
