Acres to call their own

Set on a working farm near Rusper, Club K9 has secure exercise fields as far as the eye can sniff — wide-open, fully fenced and made for proper off-lead zoomies.

There's shade under the old trees for hot days, long grass for a good sniffari, and a warm indoor barn for when the British weather does what it does best.

Hi, I'm Jo

Club K9 began back in 2015 as a little home-based labour of love — just me, a big heart for dogs, and my chocolate Labrador Bear by my side.

Today we've grown into a proper countryside retreat for dogs, but the mission hasn't changed one bit: to give every pooch the very best care imaginable, in a safe, happy and stimulating place where they can just be a dog.
