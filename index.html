Acres to call their own

Set on a working farm near Rusper, Club K9 has secure exercise fields as far as the eye can sniff — wide-open, fully fenced and made for proper off-lead zoomies.

There's shade under the old trees for hot days, long grass for a good sniffari, and a warm indoor barn for when the British weather does what it does best.

Membership & pricing

Day care at Club K9 runs on a simple fixed monthly membership, based on how many days a week your dog joins us. No contracts, no joining fee, no surprises.

From only £42 a day
1

Choose your days

Pick the days that suit your week — anything from one day to five. Minimum one day a week, so your dog settles into a proper routine and gets to know their group.

2

Their place is held

Those days are reserved for your dog every single week. No scrambling for a space in the school holidays, and no being turned away when we're full.

3

One fixed monthly fee

The same amount leaves your account each month, spread evenly across the year — so there's nothing to work out and no bumper bill after a long month.

Hi, I'm Jo

Club K9 began back in 2015 as a little home-based labour of love — just me, a big heart for dogs, and my chocolate Labrador Bear by my side.

Today we've grown into a proper countryside retreat for dogs, but the mission hasn't changed one bit: to give every pooch the very best care imaginable, in a safe, happy and stimulating place where they can just be a dog.
